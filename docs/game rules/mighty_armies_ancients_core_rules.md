## Welcome

Welcome to Mighty Armies: Ancients, the game of clashing hordes, hollow defeats, and resounding victories! Based on the original Mighty Armies Fantasy Game. Each battle takes around twenty or thirty minutes. Played using 15mm scale figures.

---

## Before Play (p.4)

### Playing Area
Any flat surface. Standard games need a space **2 feet by 3 feet**. Players choose opposite sides.

### Terrain
Each player may place up to **two terrain pieces** anywhere in the playing area before the game begins. Not required, but enhances play.

### Building an Army
- Standard game: two opposing forces of **60 Army Points (AP)** each.
- Total AP of all units must not exceed 60.
- All units should belong to the same time period (mercenaries/units from different nations allowed).
- Only **one unit** may have the General special ability.
- Only **four scouts** per army.

### Basing an Army
- Standard basing: **50mm × 25mm** (smaller units) or **50mm × 50mm** (larger units).
- Any base size is acceptable as long as both opponents use the same size.

### Deploying Armies
1. Both players roll a d6 (re-roll ties). **Lowest** scorer places his army first, within **6"** of his table edge.
2. Opponent then places his entire army, also within 6" of his table edge.
3. Both roll again (re-roll ties). **Highest** scorer gets the first turn.

## Turn Sequence (p.5)

Each turn, in order:
1. Roll for Move Points
2. Move your units
3. Conduct any shooting attacks
4. Fight any close combats

Then it is your opponent's turn.

### Move Points
Roll a **d6** at the start of your turn. Result = Move Points (MP) for this turn.

**1 MP costs:**
- Move a single unit or Group
- Use a Command

Rules:
- Each unit may only be moved **once per turn**.
- No limit on command uses per unit (if MP available).
- A General can only try to use a command **once on a given unit**.
- MP not spent are lost; they cannot be saved.

### Groups
- Two or more units in **base contact** (touching along one complete base edge) may form a Group.
- Moving a Group costs **1 MP** regardless of how many units it contains (up to 4 units).
- Maximum group size: **6 units**, arranged no more than **3 abreast and 2 deep**. All must face the same direction.
- An individual unit may leave a Group at any time (costs 1 MP, moves away normally).
- A Group may split into smaller Groups (each must maintain complete base contact).

### Large Groups
- Groups of **5 or 6 units** cost **2 MPs** to move.

### Large Bases
- Units with large bases (chariots, huge animals) count as **two normal bases** for Group construction.
- Maximum 3 large-base units side by side in a Group.
- Normal and large bases may be mixed in a Group.

## Movement (p.6)

- At cost of 1 MP, a unit/Group moves up to its **Speed score** (in inches).
- A unit/Group may only move **once per turn**.
- A Group always moves at the speed of its **slowest unit**.
- A unit/Group may turn up to **90°** before moving and a further **90°** after completing movement. Movement between turns must be in a **straight line**.
- A unit/Group always **pivots around its centre** when turning.
- If any part of a unit/Group **touches or moves over terrain**, remaining Speed is **halved** for that turn.
- You may **never move one unit through another** unit (including friendly units).
- You may measure any distance on the battlefield **before** moving a unit.

### Reform
At cost of 1 MP, choose **one** of the following (unit may not move normally afterwards; terrain has no effect):
- Move a unit or Group **1"** in any direction.
- Turn a unit or every unit in a group **180°**.
- Turn an entire Group **180°**, maintaining formation.
- Move one or more units **away from the rest of the group**.
- Rearrange units within the group in any new order (same facing kept).
- Move any units within **1"** of the group into the Group (placed anywhere within group, following normal composition rules).

## Combat (p.7)

### Shooting
- Units with the Shooting ability may make **one shooting attack** after all movement is completed. **No MP cost.**
- Target must be within **line of sight** (no obstructions; cannot see through terrain unless already inside it).
- Target must be **in front** of the shooting unit.
- Maximum range: **7"** from front base edge.
- Cannot shoot at a unit **locked in close combat** or part of a Group locked in close combat.
- To resolve: cross-reference attacker's Shooting rating vs target's Fighting score, roll d6. Equal to or above the target number = target **destroyed** (remove immediately).

#### Shooting Table

| Target's Fighting Score | 0–2 | 3–4 | 5+ |
|------------------------|-----|-----|-----|
| Shooting I             | 5+  | 6+  | —   |
| Shooting II            | 4+  | 5+  | 6+  |
| Artillery I            | 3+  | 4+  | 5+  |
| Artillery II           | 2+  | 3+  | 4+  |

### Initiating Close Combat
- Move a unit/Group into **base contact** with an enemy using **straight-line movement only** (not via a turn, not via a Reform).
- Once in contact, units must be **aligned**: long base edges matched against long base edges where possible. Alignment is for clarity only — not for tactical advantage.
- This alignment applies to Groups as well.

### Flank Attacks
- If you contact the enemy's **short base edge** (rather than a long edge or corner), it is a **flank attack**.
- Align your unit flat against the enemy's short base edge.

## Close Combat (p.8–9)

Close combat occurs after all MP are spent and movement is complete. Any unit with its front base edge in contact with an enemy **automatically fights**.

The player whose turn it is decides the **order** in which multiple combats are resolved.

### Single Unit vs Single Unit
Both players roll a d6 and add their unit's **Fighting score** plus relevant modifiers from the Close Combat Table.

- **Victory**: roller scores higher than opponent but less than double → enemy **Driven Back**.
- **Massacre**: roller scores double or more → enemy **destroyed** (remove immediately).
- **Draw**: same total → both remain locked in combat; neither may move away; both **automatically fight** next turn.

### Close Combat Table

| Situation | Modifier |
|-----------|----------|
| Charged – moved into close combat this turn | +1 |
| Difficult Ground – moved into terrain this turn | −1 |
| Flanked – enemy unit in contact with short or rear base edge | −1 |
| Surprise Attack – in contact with enemy's rear long base edge | +1 |

### Groups in Close Combat
- Designate one unit in the Group as the **primary fighter** (must be in base contact with the enemy).
- All other units in the Group **support** the primary fighter (add each unit's Support score).
- A new primary fighter may be selected each turn if desired.
- Resolution: roll d6, add primary fighter's Fighting score + modifiers + Support scores of all other units in the Group.

**Results for Groups:**
- **Victory** (higher but less than double): automatically destroy **one unit** of the losing player's choice; **drive the rest back**.
- **Massacre** (double or more): roll a d6 — that many units destroyed (losing player's choice); remainder Driven Back.
- **Draw**: both sides remain in place; no units lost.

Every unit/Group in contact with an enemy **must fight each turn**. A unit may be forced to fight more than once if it is in contact with multiple enemies.

### Rearrangement
After close combat, players may (not compulsory) rearrange Groups that fought:
- **Victorious Group**: owning player positions units freely, provided one remains stationary and the rest are placed around it following Group rules.
- **Group with destroyed units (not Driven Back)**: owning player may move **one unit** to replace a destroyed one (the moved unit must not already be in base contact with an enemy).
- **Exception — Flanked/Surprise Attack with no enemy at front**: Group may only be turned so its front base edges face the flanking/surprise attack enemy. Cannot do this if just Driven Back.

### Driven Back
A unit/Group that loses close combat but is not destroyed is **Driven Back**:
1. Turned **180°** immediately.
2. Moved directly forwards a distance equal to the **speed of the slowest unit**, without further turning.
3. Terrain halves this movement as normal.

If the unit/Group **cannot retreat** the full distance (blocked by friendly or enemy units, or a table edge) → **automatically destroyed**. A group need only be partially blocked for this to apply.

> **Note:** A unit/Group suffering a Surprise Attack (enemy on rear base edge) that is Driven Back is automatically destroyed, as it cannot move through the enemy. This is the tactical reward of successfully manoeuvring behind the enemy.

## Victory (p.10)

### Victory Conditions
Two ways to win:

1. **Destroy half** of your opponent's total number of units (round up — e.g. vs 9 units, destroy 5) before he does the same to you.
2. **Destroy the enemy General** and have lost fewer AP worth of units than your opponent.

Victory is declared as soon as either condition is met.
